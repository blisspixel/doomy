# The scrap league

Who actually runs the fights. The station broadcasts the league; the league is not the station.

## What it is

A scrap league is not a sport in the way a sport is organised, and it is organised in every way a sport is organised before anyone admits that is what has happened. There are venues, which are old ranges with the power still on. There are regulars. There are grudges with histories, and cheese strategies that get a nickname within a week and stop working within two. Somebody sells tickets, and there is genuine disagreement about who that somebody is.

Frags pay. Not well, and not reliably, and the accounting is the sort of accounting that happens in a place with no address. But a good night pays for the night, and that is the entire economy the league needs to keep existing.

The league denies existing, which is a courtesy everyone extends to everyone else.

## The rhythm

Warmup, then live, then the podium, then the next one loads. That is the loop and it has not changed.

Between rounds there is a bell. Everything stops for it. Nobody organised that; it is just what happens when a sound arrives in a room full of people who are all doing the same thing.

## The tribes

Nobody picks a faction. You notice the aesthetic tribes if you watch long enough, and then you cannot stop noticing them.

**Night Watch** wear black tactical with no insignia and go by callsign only. They favour the rail. They move like they are still on an operation that ended years ago and nobody told them. Their tags come up in the killfeed in capitals with no punctuation, and they mark a frag with a two-letter grid coordinate rather than a taunt, which reads as either enormous discipline or enormous affectation depending on how your night is going.

**Static Kids** run a glitch aesthetic, deliberately corrupted, with names missing their vowels. They spray scatter and they push. Their corpse markers blink for half a second longer than they should, which everyone agrees is a rendering bug except the Static Kids, who agree it is a feature.

**Hangar Candy** are glossy black-triangle drip and smooth operators, flechette mains because it is the most reliable thing in the room. They leave a DENIED watermark on a frag and their taunts read like declassification stamps. You either enjoy them or you think they are trying too hard, and they are entirely comfortable with which one you picked.

**Gulf Breeze** are believers. Their tags reference old sightings and old coordinates, and they play like they are testing the map boundaries for something that was not supposed to be in them. Their favourite positions are corners where two wall textures do not quite line up. Sometimes the killfeed shows coordinates instead of a name when they get a frag. Probably a bug.

These are how people look, not what they believe. A Night Watch player can be a Pourer. Nobody has ever found that strange.

## The Sweep

The league's other format, and the one people get quiet about.

A sweep does not have a winner. Things come, and they keep coming, and the arithmetic of how many come is not in your favour and was never meant to be. You do not win a sweep. You last in one, and the number you last is the thing people remember, and the thing people are actually competing over is how long.

It is the most popular night of the week and nobody at the venue will explain why.
