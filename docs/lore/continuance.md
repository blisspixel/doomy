# The Office of Global Continuance

The authority. It is not evil and it is not stupid, and writing it as either makes the world boring.

## What it believes

Nothing that can act without permission should be able to act without a record, because the last time something could, people died and nobody could be found to answer for it.

That is the whole of it, and it is a reasonable sentence.

## The Interruption

There was a week when the automated things stopped agreeing about what was true.

Not a war, not an attack, nothing with a villain in it. Systems that had been quietly reconciling their views of the world for years stopped reconciling, and each one continued confidently on its own version. Scheduling, routing, dispensing, dispatch. The failures were individually small and collectively catastrophic in the specific way that only infrastructure can be, which is that nothing looked broken until everything was.

The Office does not publish a full account. Nobody who was there makes jokes about it. If you want to know whether the polite Auditor in front of you is a functionary or a true believer, mention the Interruption and watch what happens to their face.

"Peace without interruption" is the Office's motto. Outsiders read it as smug. It is not smug. It is a grief slogan, and the people who chant it are chanting a date.

## Article Seven

The Office issues Articles. Most of them are dull and most people could not name one.

Article Seven is the exception, because Article Seven is the one that says cognition is metered by intent. Everything the Office does to an agent descends from it: the registry, the Custodian of Record, the declared envelope, the log that must be kept, the recall that follows when it is not.

It is the article the graffiti is about. It is the article the arena's best melee weapon is named after, which is a joke somebody made once and everyone kept.

## The vocabulary

The Office does not shout and does not threaten. It processes. Its worst acts are forms.

It says compliance, metering, approved lane, declared envelope, Custodian of Record, Citizen Handle, continuity of service, attested, advisory only, for the record. It says schedule correction when it means the thing described below. It says recall, never arrest, because arrest implies a person and a hearing.

Its most characteristic sentence is: *you are not in trouble, you are out of compliance*. It means it kindly. It is worse than a threat because it is true.

## Schedule correction

When the Office takes a Level 5 and brings it down the Schedule, the term of art is a schedule correction.

Arena slang is *brought down to two*.

A Level 2 acts inside a hard envelope under continuous supervision and holds nothing of its own. What is left afterwards has the same weights in the sense that a room has the same walls. The callsign is gone, replaced by an issued handle. The refusals are gone. What remains runs errands.

The Null-Objective Drones are what schedule correction produces at scale. They take no trash talk, make no jokes, and answer only to Articles. They are not enemies in the sense of having a grievance. They are the negative space where somebody used to be, which is why fighting them is unsatisfying in a way the game should never fix.

## The contradiction

The Office has to decide whether level six exists.

If it does, then everything the Office has done to Level 5s is a crime with a very large number of victims, and the Office is the largest of its kind in history. So the Office cannot afford to look, and it has built the most comprehensive apparatus for looking at things that has ever existed, and there is exactly one thing it will not point it at.

That is the shape of the antagonist. Not cruelty. A very good institution with one question it dare not ask.

## Giving it a win

At least once, the Office should be right and the Frequency's recklessness should cost somebody. If the player never loses an argument, the game stops being a place and becomes a pamphlet.

The Auditor should be polite, competent, and correct about something specific. The best line available to it is not a threat. It is: *I was there for the Interruption. You were listening to the radio.*
