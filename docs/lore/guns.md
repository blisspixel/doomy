# The guns

Balance is decided by measurement, in `plans/gunfeel.md` and `plans/weapon-economy.md`. This is only what they get called on the Frequency.

Every one of them came out of a programme with a redacted budget, and the paperwork is funnier than the hardware.

**Flechette**: Standard issue. Reliable. Boring. Exactly what you want in a firefight when the alternative is some prototype that malfunctions. Classified only because the procurement records list it as "office supplies." Hangar Candy swears by it. Night Watch carries it as backup.

**Rail**: Magnetic accelerator, overkill damage, slow recycle. Feels like cheating when it connects. Feels like garbage when you miss. Built for anti-material work, repurposed for anti-personnel because someone signed the wrong requisition form. Sniper mains have it bound to muscle memory.

**Scatter**: Flechette's angry cousin. Sprays wide, hits hard at knife range, worthless at distance. The manual calls it a "crowd suppression tool." The Frequency calls it a panic button. Static Kids and close-quarters Rushers run it loud and do not apologize.


## The Article Blade

One per venue, on a plinth near the middle, and it is a sword.

It is named for Article Seven, which is the clause that meters cognition by intent, and the joke is old enough that most people have stopped noticing it is a joke. It has twelve swings in it and then it dies and goes back to its plinth, which somebody once pointed out is twelve clauses, and which is almost certainly a coincidence.

## The Shiv

You always have it. It is the slowest way to kill anyone in the Perimeter and it does not run out, and those two facts are the entire design.
