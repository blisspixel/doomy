# Map Callouts

Standard location names for the default arena. Used for communication and future map overlay UI.

## Arena Zones

### Central
- **HUB** - Central open area, main combat zone
- **CHOKE** - Narrow passage connecting two major areas

### Vertical
- **PIT** - Lower elevation depression
- **HIGH** - Elevated platform or ledge

### Spawn Locations
- **SPAWN A** - First team/bot spawn point
- **SPAWN B** - Second team/bot spawn point

### Weapon/Item Areas
- **RAIL ALCOVE** - Recessed area near rail weapon pickup (future)
- **ROCKET RAMP** - Angled approach to rocket weapon area (future)

### Resource
- **HEALTH NOOK** - Small corner/alcove with health pickup (future)

## Future Expansion

As map complexity increases, additional callouts can follow this naming pattern:
- Directional: NORTH HALL, SOUTH BRIDGE
- Descriptive: GLASS ROOM, TOWER, TUNNEL
- Gameplay: SNIPER PERCH, JUMP PAD, TELEPORTER

Keep names short (1-2 words), memorable, and unambiguous.
